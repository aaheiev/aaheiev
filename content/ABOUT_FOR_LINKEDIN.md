# Principal / Platform Engineer | Cloud Infrastructure | DevOps & SRE | DevSecOps | FinTech

I've been building and running infrastructure since 2002. What started as a Support Engineer role 
at a small ISP in Odesa, Ukraine, while studying at Odesa State Polytechnic University, gradually 
evolved into a career spanning infrastructure, cloud engineering, DevOps, SRE, platform engineering, 
and security.

A few milestones along the way:

**2002–2005 | The foundations**

Started with Linux systems, networking, and database administration. By 2003, I was managing the core database 
behind a telecom billing system.

**2005–2010 | Private cloud and automation**

Worked on solving the challenges of managing development and QA environments for large engineering teams. 
This led to building our own private cloud using VMware and HP hardware.

**2010–2016 | Startups and infrastructure ownership**

Worked with several startups, taking care of everything from hosting, DNS, and TLS certificates to application 
infrastructure. Mainly Nginx, PHP, Node.js, and MySQL.

**2016–2018 | Public cloud and containers**

Worked on migrating Google Nest's development infrastructure from AWS to Google Cloud. Later moved into 
containerization and Kubernetes.

**2020–2023 | FinTech, DevOps & Security**

Relocated to the Netherlands and joined the FinTech industry. Expanded my focus beyond DevOps and SRE into 
security engineering, compliance, and integrating security into development and infrastructure workflows.

**2023–Present | Principal Engineering & Platform Architecture**

Designed and built the infrastructure and DevOps foundation for a multi-tenant, multi-environment banking 
integration platform. Took it from an initial PoC to a production platform with SOC 2 certification.

---

Over 20+ years, I've seen infrastructure evolve from physical servers and traditional hosting to private clouds, 
public clouds, containers, Kubernetes, and cloud-native platforms.

Technology keeps changing, but what I enjoy most remains the same: getting hands-on, solving complex engineering 
problems, building reliable platforms, and making life easier for development teams.

And I'm still enjoying the journey.
