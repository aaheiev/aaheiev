# My Professional Journey

I started my career in 2002 as a Support Engineer at a small ISP in Odesa, Ukraine, while studying at 
Odesa State Polytechnic University.

In 2003, I took responsibility for my first database — the core of a telecom billing system.

In 2005, I started solving the challenges of managing and refreshing multiple development and QA environments 
for a large R&D team. This eventually led us to build our own private cloud using VMware and HP hardware.

In 2010, I started working with startups, taking care of everything needed to get their applications online: 
hosting, domains, DNS, TLS certificates, and application infrastructure. Mostly Nginx, PHP, Node.js, and MySQL.

In 2016, I worked on migrating Google Nest's development infrastructure from AWS to Google Cloud.

In 2018, I moved into containers and Kubernetes.

In 2020, I relocated to the Netherlands and joined the FinTech industry. Alongside DevOps and SRE, I became 
increasingly involved in security and integrating it into infrastructure and development processes.

In 2023, I took on a Principal Engineer role, designing and building the infrastructure and DevOps foundation 
for a multi-tenant banking integration platform — taking it from PoC to a production platform with SOC 2 certification.

Over the years, I've gone from managing Linux servers and databases to building private and public clouds, 
Kubernetes platforms, and modern DevOps and DevSecOps environments.

I still enjoy getting hands-on, solving complex problems, figuring out how things work, and building things 
that make engineers' lives easier.
