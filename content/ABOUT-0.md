# My Professional Journey

I started my career in 2002 as a support engineer of a small ISP in Odesa, Ukraine during my
studies at Odesa State Polytechnic University.
In 2003 I've been managing my first database - a core of a billing system for a small ISP.
In 2005 I started solving problems of a periodic refres of multiple dev and qa environments for a large team
of R&D and QA engineers. Then started think about a cloud and we built our own private cloud based on VMware
and HP hardware.
In 2010 I started worked with multiple startups. They gave me their applications and I run them in internet.
Hosting, DNS domains and DNS, TLS certificates. Mostly Nginx, PHP, Node.js, MySQL
In 2016 I migrated Google Nest development infrastructure from AWS to Google Cloud.
In 2018 I did my first migration to containers and then to Kubenretes.
In 2020 I moved to The Netherlands and started working with FinTech companies.
In addition to DevOps and SRE I started to work with implementation of security.
In 2023 I tried myself as s a Principal Engineer and I designed and implemented infrastructure and devops
for multi tenant, multi-environment banking integration platform from PoC to SOC2 certified platform.
In 2026 I started working with consolidated type of trading data which uses low latency network connections to stock exchanges.
