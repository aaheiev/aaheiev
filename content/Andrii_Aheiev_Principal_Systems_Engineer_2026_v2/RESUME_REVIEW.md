The CV includes strong target-role keywords such as Kubernetes, Terraform, cloud, observability, security, and CI/CD across multiple sections.

The background in fintech and multi-tenant platform environments is relevant for principal-level infrastructure roles.

### What to improve
The summary does not clearly position the candidate as a technical leader or principal engineer, so recruiters may read this as a senior DevOps profile rather than a staff/principal IC.
Several bullets describe responsibilities but not the business or engineering impact, which weakens differentiation for principal-level screening.
The CV lacks explicit architecture, scaling, reliability, or cross-team influence language that principal/staff hiring managers expect.
The contact/header area uses creative branding and mixed separators, which slightly reduces ATS cleanliness and scan speed.
